AI Syllabus Statements

From the Provost's AI Committee Student Subcommittee

Your instructor chooses one of four statements for the course, or for each assignment. Check your syllabus to see which one applies.

Most restrictiveMost open
  1. 1

    AI Prohibited

    No AI use

    No AI tools on any work in this course.

    Why

    So you can show your own understanding and skills.

  2. 2

    AI Planning

    Limited use

    AI to get started, not to finish.

    You may

    Plan, brainstorm, develop ideas, and research.

    You must

    Turn in the tool, your prompt, and its results with the assignment.

  3. 3

    AI Refinement

    Limited use

    The ideas are yours. AI helps polish them.

    You may

    Proofread or review work you have drafted.

    You must

    Generate your own ideas, and cite any AI you use.

  4. 4

    Full Use

    AI permitted

    AI on any assignment, with credit.

    You may

    Use AI tools throughout the course.

    You must

    Document and cite every use of AI.

Going beyond a statement

Using AI beyond what a statement allows may be academic misconduct. It can lead to a failing grade and a referral to the Office of Student Conduct.

Not sure? Ask first.

Ask your instructor before you submit. How to cite AI: libguides.wku.edu/stylewrite/ai
